The problem

Three reasons a website earns nothing.

Nobody finds it

A site that doesn't come up when someone searches your trade plus your city name is an expense, not an investment. Your Google listing and the structure of your pages usually matter more than the design.

Nobody knows what to do on it

A visitor who sees no prices, no buttons and no obvious way to reach you leaves. Every page should have exactly one thing it asks people to do, and it should say so plainly.

It isn't yours

Plenty of businesses are renting their website without realising it: stop paying and everything disappears. Here, you own the domain name, the code and your copy from day one.
